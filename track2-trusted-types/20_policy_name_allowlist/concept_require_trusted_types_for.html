Vocabulary Web · The Trusted Types Directive FamilyConcept: require-trusted-types-for

This is the ON SWITCH. A CSP directive whose only defined value is the keyword 'script' — present or absent, nothing in between. Its entire job is: "every guarded sink on this page must now receive branded values, not raw strings."

What it looks like

Content-Security-Policy: require-trusted-types-for 'script'

Where you've seen it

  • Module 15 — introduced; with zero policies defined, EVERY guarded sink throws unconditionally
  • Modules 16–19 — the constant background condition every proof in those modules ran under
  • Module 24 — present in the final, real, shippable policy

What it does NOT do

It does not create any policy, authorize any policy name, or provide any sanitization logic itself. It's purely a switch — flipping it on with zero other Trusted Types setup makes every guarded sink permanently broken (Module 15's exact finding), not permissive.

How this connects to the other terms require-trusted-types-for turns ON enforcement. trusted-types (a DIFFERENT directive, next page) restricts WHICH policy names may then be created to satisfy that enforcement. Confusing the two is this vocabulary web's whole reason for existing — see the connection hub.
